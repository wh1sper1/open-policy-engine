# Contributing to Open Policy Engine

Welcome. Our mission is to convert speculative tech-authoritarianism narratives into disciplined, citation-backed legal and policy defense. We operate as an **Evidence Machine, Not an Echo Chamber**.

## 1. The Core Rule: Fact Over Motive
We do not accept contributions based on "vibes," speculative YouTube commentary, or unverified conspiracy theories.

## 2. Taxonomy Standard
All Pull Requests must tag assertions with `[VERIFIED]`, `[PLAUSIBLE]`, or `[ALLEGED]`.
`[VERIFIED]` claims MUST include direct hyperlinks to primary source documents (statutes, court dockets, vendor specs, SEC/FEC filings).
